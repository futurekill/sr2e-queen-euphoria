## Insect Hive  (npc + race:"spirit"; from *Insects Among Us*, book p.54)
QE prints only the **rules** for its ants, not a manifest attribute table
(p.55 is blank Hive condition-monitors). So model **Soldier** and **Worker** ant
spirits **Force-scaled**, the SR2 insect-spirit convention the plan anticipated
(attributes derive from Force). The **Queen is a non-combat set-piece** (book:
"does not actually appear") — one display actor, no combat stats.

Build for each of Soldier and Worker: a **True Form** and a **Flesh Form** actor
(4 combat actors total). Verified rules to bake into the bio as **MANUAL** GM
notes (the NPC workflow won't enforce them):
- Astral: stats from Force; **+5 initiative** to Reaction in astral (attack/
  defend only, no powers). Manifest: use the Force-scaled block.
- Manifest: **Immunity to Normal Weapons** vs *all ranged* attacks (not spells);
  **armor = 2 × Force**.
- Melee vs a manifested spirit uses **Willpower**, not weapon skill (magic /
  Vulnerability attacks exempt).
- **Flesh Form** (spirit in a human host, dual being, cannot astrally project):
  **Soldier** adds its Force to the host's Physical attributes; **Worker**
  reduces the host's Physical by 1; both set Mental attributes to the spirit's
  True Form. Defeat by killing the body, banishing, or astral combat.
- Soldier = aggressive/fearless; Worker = cowardly, screeches to warn the Hive,
  and sacrifices itself if the cocoons or Queen are threatened.
- Suggested Forces for the module (GM scales to taste): Workers Force 2–3,
  Soldiers Force 3–4, per the DE ant precedent.
